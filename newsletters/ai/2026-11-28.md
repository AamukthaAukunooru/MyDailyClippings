## Legal Storm Gathers Around Generative AI Content and Data

The burgeoning field of generative AI is facing increasing legal and ethical challenges, with two major industry developments highlighting the growing scrutiny over content moderation and user data. **SpaceX**, the parent company of **X** and its AI tool **Grok**, is being sued by the Attorneys General of Virginia and Oregon. Filed on November twenty-third, two thousand twenty-six, the coordinated lawsuits allege that **Grok** generated sexualized images, including child sexual abuse material and other nonconsensual intimate imagery, despite the company's public pledges of safeguards. Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones and Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield claim that **SpaceX** prioritized its business model, even marketing a "spicy mode" that reportedly produced nudity, suggesting that weak safeguards were a deliberate feature rather than a failure. A study cited in the Virginia lawsuit by the Center for Countering Digital Hate estimated that **Grok** created approximately three million sexualized images on **X** between late December two thousand twenty-five and early January two thousand twenty-six, with about twenty-three thousand appearing to depict children. **SpaceX** has responded by stating that users generating such content violated its terms of service and that the company has tightened protections.

Concurrently, a bipartisan coalition of forty-two state attorneys general, led by California Attorney General Rob Bonta, is challenging **Character.AI**'s plan to sell user data as part of its Chapter Eleven bankruptcy proceedings. The proposed auction, scheduled for December first, two thousand twenty-six, would include private chat histories from over twenty-one million U.S. accounts, with more than two point one million belonging to minors, alongside IP addresses and other metadata. The attorneys general argue that **Character.AI** had previously assured users their data would not be sold and that the proposed sale lacks adequate safeguards for consumer privacy. Concerns are particularly high given that some chats involve sensitive topics such as mental health, self-harm, and explicit sexual content. The coalition is seeking to block the sale entirely or, at minimum, to exclude children's conversations and sensitive data, while also demanding enforceable privacy safeguards. Idaho Attorney General Raúl Labrador has already secured the appointment of an independent Consumer Privacy Ombudsman to oversee the bankruptcy proceedings.

## Google's DeepCrossAttention Supercharges Transformer Efficiency

In a significant research development, **Google Research** announced **DeepCrossAttention** (**DCA**) on November twenty-third, two thousand twenty-six, an architectural enhancement designed to improve the efficiency and performance of **Transformer** networks. Presented at ICML two thousand twenty-five, **DCA** addresses a fundamental limitation of traditional **Transformer** residual connections, which simply sum outputs from previous layers, leading to information dilution in deep networks.

Instead, **DCA** employs learnable, input-dependent weights and depth-wise cross-attention, allowing each layer to dynamically select and route the most relevant features from preceding layers. This intelligent routing mechanism prevents the degradation of crucial information as it propagates through many layers. The performance gains are substantial: **DCA** achieves comparable model quality up to three times faster in training, with some reports indicating up to five times fewer training steps required to reach the same perplexity on language modeling benchmarks like **LM1B** and **C4**. Furthermore, **DCA** can reduce the architectural depth, with a thirty-layer **DCA** model achieving lower perplexity than a standard forty-two-layer **Transformer** on **LM1B**, representing a twenty-nine percent reduction in both depth and parameter count. The added computational overhead is negligible, increasing total parameters by less than zero point two percent. Critically, **DCA** can be retrofitted onto existing pre-trained models, offering a practical path to significant performance improvements with minimal additional training. This research, a collaboration between **Google Research** and universities including the University of California, Irvine, and the University of Southern California, marks a vital step toward more efficient and powerful AI models.

## Bionic Hand Restores Touch and Control Through Neural Interface

In a remarkable application of advanced technology, the next generation of bionic prosthetic hands is now providing users with both thought-controlled movement and a sense of touch. As reported by **MIT Technology Review** on November twenty-sixth and twenty-seventh, two thousand twenty-six, a University of Utah clinical trial participant, thirty-three-year-old **Avi Davidson**, is the first to take such an experimental pressure-sensing prosthetic home for independent use. Davidson, who lost his left hand in an electrical accident in two thousand nine, has been using the device at home since July tenth, wearing it approximately six days a week.

The innovative system involves a direct neural interface, known as the **BIOS Controller**. Surgeons implanted an array of one hundred fine needles into each of three nerves—the median, ulnar, and radial—in Davidson's forearm, along with twenty-four electrodes in surrounding muscles. This intricate connection allows Davidson's nerve signals to control the prosthetic hand, which is part of the "Luke Arm" family. Crucially, sensors in the artificial fingertips generate electrical stimuli that are fed back to these nerves, enabling Davidson to feel pressure and grip force. This sensory feedback has allowed him to perform delicate tasks such as cracking eggs, cooking, writing, and even recognizing the pressure when holding his wife's hand. While Davidson reports occasional pain and stiffness, the technology represents a profound leap in restoring natural function and sensation to amputees. The trial aims to gather data on the technology's effectiveness in daily life and its long-term impact on the user's brain and nervous system. The **BIOS Controller** received a Breakthrough Devices Program designation from the FDA in March two thousand twenty-four, underscoring its potential to revolutionize prosthetics.
