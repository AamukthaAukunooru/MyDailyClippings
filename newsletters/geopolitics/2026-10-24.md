## Middle East and European Security

The **Middle East** has experienced a surge in violence, with **Israel** launching a large-scale military operation in the **West Bank** following an attack that killed two soldiers. The Israeli army initiated a "targeted" operation in **Hebron** and **Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu** ordered forces to go "house to house" across the **West Bank**. This escalation comes amidst heightened regional instability.

Further south, a deadly attack occurred at an Orthodox church funeral in the southern **Karak** province of **Jordan**, where gunmen opened fire, killing at least seven people and injuring thirty-four. Officials have described the incident as a terrorist act, motivated by extremist ideology, and a manhunt for the perpetrators is underway.

In **Europe**, **France** also faced a security incident when a gunman opened fire randomly near **Paris**, killing one person and wounding five others in a series of attacks southwest of the capital. The suspected gunman was apprehended, and **French President Emmanuel Macron** declared the deadly shooting a "racist terrorist attack". French government officials are now planning to bolster police presence across the country, anticipating new protests and aiming to prevent a repeat of recent unrest that injured police officers and damaged stores.

## Developments in the Ukraine Conflict

The conflict between **Ukraine** and **Russia** continues to see both military actions and diplomatic efforts. The **White House** announced that **Melania Trump** brokered a truce for the **Ukrainian** city of **Oleshky**, which was reportedly on the brink of disaster. Both **Ukraine** and **Russia** have confirmed this truce in the Russian-occupied city, where residents had been forced to consume garden weeds for survival.

However, other aspects of the conflict remain contentious. Reports emerged regarding the status of **Starlink** satellite internet services in **Ukraine**. While **former US President Donald Trump** stated that limits on the network had been lifted, **Ukraine** maintains that **Starlink** remains restricted. The network is crucial for the **Ukrainian** military, and the conflicting reports highlight ongoing complexities in technological support for the war effort.

Militarily, **Ukraine** has continued its strategy of drone strikes against **Russia**, with repeated attacks increasingly disrupting operations at **Moscow's** airports. These drone attacks are part of **Ukraine's** broader plan to shut down **Russia's** airspace. Meanwhile, **Russia's** attacks on **Ukraine's** capital, **Kyiv**, are reportedly intensifying, making 2026 one of the bloodiest years for the city in the long-running conflict.

## Global Health and Regional Challenges

In global health, the **World Health Organization (WHO)** has announced the commencement of a clinical trial for an **Ebola** vaccine in the **Democratic Republic of Congo**. This trial aims to test the safety, efficacy, and immunogenicity of multiple vaccines against the deadly virus. The announcement comes as the **Ebola** outbreak has spread to four countries across eastern, central, and western **Africa**. **Kenya** recently confirmed its second **Ebola** death, though authorities stated there is "no evidence" of community spread within the country.

Elsewhere, **Haiti's** new **Gang Suppression Force** is facing its first major crisis after an ambush left four **Salvadoran** soldiers missing. This incident escalates the challenges for the mission, which has already had a rocky start in its efforts to combat gang violence in the Caribbean nation.

In **Afghanistan**, concerns are growing that **Afghan** women could lose one of their last remaining avenues for higher education. The **United States** has reportedly defunded one of the last universities providing **Afghan** women with the opportunity to receive an education following the **Taliban** takeover in 2021. A student interviewed by **DW** expressed a loss of hope for ever earning a degree, highlighting the severe impact on women's rights and access to education in the country.

Finally, **US Secretary of State Marco Rubio** urged **Cubans** seeking political change to act, stating that "now is the time". These comments were made shortly after **former President Trump** suggested at a US rally that warships could intervene upon their return from **Iran**.
