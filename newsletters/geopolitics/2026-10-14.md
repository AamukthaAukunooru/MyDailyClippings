Critics argue that this deal, which temporarily authorizes certain transactions involving **Russian**-origin diesel fuel through April 7, 2027, would provide financial support to **Moscow**, enabling its ongoing military actions in **Ukraine**. This move by **President Trump** has been characterized as "squeezing **Ukraine**" and putting **Kyiv** in a difficult position as winter approaches, particularly given a proposed one-sided ceasefire plan. **European** allies have also expressed concern, with some **Ukrainian** allies in **Poland** noting a "very difficult few days" diplomatically following the deal. **President Trump** stated the deal would benefit **Americans** and the world by lowering diesel prices, especially for farmers, ranchers, and truckers. However, analysts question whether **Russia** can deliver the promised volumes given damage to its refineries, and some experts suggest the quantities are too small to significantly impact **US** prices.

In a separate development, **Russia**'s FSB security agency detained **Imai Yuki**, a **Japanese** journalist from the *Hokkaido Shimbun* newspaper, on the island of **Sakhalin**, accusing him of collecting information about **Russia**’s defenses and alleging spying. The detention occurred the day before he planned to leave **Russia**, and he was subsequently transferred to custody in **Moscow**.

In **Germany**, a **Ukrainian** national, **Serhii K.**, is on trial, accused of being involved in a plot to destroy the **Nord Stream** pipelines in September 2022. Prosecutors allege he was an officer in the **Ukrainian** army at the time and helped plan the operation with other military personnel "on behalf of state authorities in **Ukraine**". The objective, according to prosecutors, was to halt **Russian** gas deliveries and prevent **Moscow** from using energy revenues to finance its war against **Ukraine**.

Meanwhile, **Kimberly Guilfoyle** has resigned from her position as the **US** ambassador to **Greece**, denying allegations of misconduct. Her resignation follows intense scrutiny and reports that she sought payment from a **Trump** donor in exchange for access to administration officials, an allegation she denies. The **White House** confirmed that **Ambassador Guilfoyle** was asked to resign.

## South Asia Internal Dynamics

**Pakistan** is facing internal challenges, with the government imposing emergency rule in **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa** province. This decision, made by **President Asif Ali Zardari** on the advice of **Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif**'s cabinet, cited growing incidents of terrorism and accused the former **PTI**-led provincial administration of failing to cooperate in combating militancy. The move has raised questions about its constitutional justification and potential to deepen political instability, particularly as it involves suspending the elected provincial government of the **Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI)** party, led by jailed former **Prime Minister Imran Khan**. Relations between **Pakistan** and **Afghanistan** remain strained over the **Tehrik-i-Taliban Pakistan (TTP)**, which **Islamabad** claims operates from sanctuaries in **Afghanistan**.

In **India**, **Prime Minister Narendra Modi**'s government is facing youth-led protests against the election chief and a contentious voter list revision. Thousands have been detained and leaders arrested, with central **New Delhi** experiencing lockdowns and police crackdowns. The protests, partly mobilized by the satirical **Cockroach Janta Party**, initially focused on mismanagement in national college-level qualifying examinations, leading to the resignation of the union education minister. The movement has since expanded to address concerns about voter disenfranchisement and has garnered broad support from opposition parties and youth groups. While the protests are unlikely to topple the **Indian** government, they represent a significant challenge to **Modi**'s political power and have mobilized a new generation of protesters.
