Here is your daily geopolitical newsletter, "My Daily Clippings," for October 25, 2026.

Today's global overview highlights significant developments across multiple regions. In **Ukraine**, the ongoing conflict continues to see both diplomatic efforts for localized ceasefires and intensified attacks, alongside discussions regarding critical digital infrastructure. Tensions remain high in the **Middle East**, with potential shifts in US policy towards **Iran** and escalating violence in the **Israeli-Palestinian** conflict. Meanwhile, key political events are unfolding in **Brazil**, security challenges persist in **Haiti**, and social unrest continues in **France**, while a new maritime agreement involving **Turkey** and **Libya** is drawing international concern.

## The Conflict in Ukraine and Digital Infrastructure

The role of satellite communication in the **Ukraine** conflict has come under scrutiny, with **Elon Musk**, owner of **Starlink**, stating that **Ukraine** had violated the service's rules. These comments followed a disruption in the country's satellite service. However, **Ukraine**'s military subsequently reported that drones utilizing **Starshield**, a Pentagon-funded service, were operating at normal speeds. The **Ukrainian** government also affirmed that the military maintains a stable connection to **Starlink**, ten days after initial reports of restrictions.

Amidst the broader conflict, a localized ceasefire in **Oleshky** has been reported. Former US President **Donald Trump** publicly praised First Lady **Melania Trump** for her role in facilitating this localized **Russia-Ukraine** ceasefire, describing her efforts as "better than professionals". Despite such localized agreements, the overall intensity of the conflict appears to be escalating in some areas. Attacks by **Russia** on **Ukraine**'s capital, **Kyiv**, are reportedly intensifying, making 2026 one of the bloodiest years for the city since the war began.

## Middle East Tensions

The **Middle East** remains a focal point of geopolitical activity. Former US President **Donald Trump** has indicated that the United States is considering a possible attack on **Iran** as early as Sunday, or alternatively, could reach a major deal with **Tehran**. This statement suggests a potential significant shift in US policy towards **Iran**.

Meanwhile, the **Israeli-Palestinian** conflict continues to see escalating violence and military operations. Following the killing of two Israeli soldiers by two Palestinian gunmen on Friday, **Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu** ordered a large-scale military operation in the **West Bank**, stating that Israeli forces would conduct "house to house" searches. This crackdown has been accompanied by the displacement of **Palestinian** families as Israeli forces demolished homes in **Hebron**. Reports also indicate that the current olive harvest in the Israeli-occupied **West Bank** is experiencing the most violence in years, with settlers reportedly attacking farmers and besieging homes. These actions have drawn condemnation from US officials and other international bodies.

## Global Political and Security Dynamics

In the **Americas**, **Brazil** is on the cusp of a pivotal presidential run-off election. Both incumbent **Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva** and challenger **Jair Bolsonaro** held final rallies, drawing thousands of supporters ahead of Sunday's vote. The election will determine whether **Brazilians** will continue with their current left-wing leadership or shift towards the right. Further north, **Haiti** is grappling with ongoing security challenges. Remains found in the country could belong to missing **Salvadoran** soldiers who were ambushed six days ago while serving as part of a **U.N.**-backed anti-gang task force. **El Salvador** had dispatched hundreds of troops to search for the missing personnel.

Across the Atlantic, **France** has experienced another wave of mass protests. While largely peaceful marches took place, clashes erupted in the evening, leading to nearly one thousand arrests. A substantial police presence and the rerouting of protest routes were credited with keeping a lid on widespread vandalism, preventing a repeat of the extensive property damage seen in previous unrest. In the Mediterranean, a new agreement between **Turkey** and **Libya** to redraw maritime boundaries as part of an economic corridor has sparked strong protests from **Greece** and drawn the **European Union** into the dispute.
