The global landscape continues to be shaped by a mix of humanitarian crises, significant diplomatic shifts, and ongoing regional instabilities. A catastrophic flood in **Nepal** has led to a major loss of life and a large number of missing persons, prompting international concern and aid efforts. In the Americas, a landmark oil agreement between the **United States** and **Venezuela** has been announced, potentially reshaping energy dynamics. Meanwhile, parts of **Africa** are experiencing renewed unrest, with reports of gunfire in **Niger**'s capital and a notable surge in piracy off the coast of **Somalia**.

## Humanitarian Crisis in South Asia

**Nepal** is grappling with the devastating aftermath of flash floods and landslides, which have resulted in a rapidly increasing number of missing individuals and widespread destruction. The Nepali disaster relief agency initially reported 1,900 people missing, with approximately 900 of those from hydropower projects. This number has since risen significantly, with reports indicating 2,478 people are now missing, and bodies have been recovered down rivers in **India**. The scale of the disaster has prompted **Nepal** to reverse an earlier decision and allow foreign teams to assist with rescue efforts. The country's booming hydropower industry has been particularly affected, with hundreds of workers still unaccounted for, some believed to be trapped in tunnels. Survivors are facing profound loss, struggling with immediate survival and uncertain futures as homes and livelihoods have been swept away. Exclusive videos have provided clearer views of the glacier collapse near **Tibet** that initiated the deadly flash floods, sending a wall of water and mud through river valleys. The Australian government has deployed additional personnel, with 41 Australians reported missing in the floods.

## Energy Diplomacy and Geopolitical Shifts in the Americas

A significant development in energy diplomacy has emerged with **United States** President **Donald Trump** announcing what he described as a "historic" and "biggest oil deal in world history" with **Venezuela**. The agreement reportedly grants the **US** control over 65 billion barrels of **Venezuela**'s proven oil reserves. **Delcy Rodríguez**, identified as **Venezuela**'s interim president, stated that the unusual agreement is expected to help revive her country's economy. This agreement comes amid a complex political landscape in **Venezuela**, which has seen internal divisions and international sanctions in recent years. The deal, if fully implemented, could have substantial implications for global energy markets and the geopolitical standing of both nations.

## Instability and Security Concerns in Africa

Security concerns have heightened in West Africa following reports of gunfire and blasts in several areas of **Niamey**, the capital of **Niger**. Shots were heard around the city's Diori Hamani international airport and near the presidential palace, indicating potential unrest or a significant security incident. The nature and scale of these events are still unfolding, but they underscore ongoing fragility in the region, which has experienced several coups and political upheavals in recent years.

Further east, off the coast of **Somalia**, there has been a notable surge in piracy. Two ships were reportedly seized within four days last week, bringing the total number of attacks to at least 13 since January. This increase in piracy is being linked to the broader impact of the **US-Iran war**, suggesting ripple effects of larger international conflicts on regional security. The resurgence of piracy poses renewed threats to international shipping lanes and maritime trade in the Gulf of Aden and the wider Indian Ocean.

In other regional developments, **Guinea-Bissau** is holding a vote on a proposed constitution that aims to grant the president more power and reshape parliament and elections. This constitutional referendum could significantly alter the political structure of the West African nation.
