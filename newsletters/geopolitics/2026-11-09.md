Today's global landscape is marked by a series of significant security developments in the Middle East, alongside evolving political dynamics in various regions. Energy infrastructure in **Iraq** has been targeted by a drone attack, while **Saudi Arabia** has faced renewed strikes from **Houthi** forces. Geopolitical tensions are also highlighted by an espionage investigation involving the CEO of US tech giant **Nvidia**. Meanwhile, internal political processes are underway in **Israel** and **South Africa**, and a notable intersection of religious and economic discourse has emerged in **Argentina**. Environmental concerns, particularly regarding air quality, are also in focus in **India** during the Diwali festival.

## Middle East Security and Political Shifts

A drone attack on **Iraq's Khor Mor gasfield** has led to a significant power outage, affecting more than 70 percent of the northern **Kurdish region**. The strike suspended production at the facility, which is a crucial source of natural gas for electricity generation in the region. This incident underscores the ongoing security challenges and the vulnerability of critical energy infrastructure in **Iraq**.

Further south, **Saudi Arabia** has experienced fresh drone strikes, with the **Aramco Tower** in the capital's central financial district reportedly hit. Footage authenticated by BBC Verify showed damage to the building. These attacks are attributed to **Houthi** forces, indicating a continuation of regional hostilities and a persistent threat to **Saudi** civilian and economic targets.

In **Israel**, the political landscape is in flux as **President Isaac Herzog** has tasked **Gadi Eisenkot**, a former military chief and leader of an anti-Netanyahu alliance, with forming a new government. **Eisenkot**, a career general and a leading rival of **Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu**, now has up to six weeks to attempt to build a coalition. This development follows a period of political uncertainty and sets the stage for potentially complex coalition negotiations.

## Geopolitical Tensions and Domestic Political Dynamics

US tech giant **Nvidia** has initiated an independent investigation following reports that its founder and CEO, **Jensen Huang**, was the target of foreign intelligence. **Mr. Huang** reportedly had a close relationship with a **Chinese** businesswoman who is suspected of passing information to **Beijing**. **Nvidia** confirmed on Sunday that it is looking into the matter, with **Mr. Huang** admitting to an affair with the woman. This probe highlights growing concerns over corporate espionage and the intersection of personal relationships with national security interests, particularly in the context of US-China technological competition.

In **Argentina**, **Pope Leo** has conveyed a message to **President Javier Milei**, emphasizing that the economy must serve the needy. The pontiff's visit comes amid tensions between the country's libertarian leader and its **Catholic Church**, which is deeply committed to social justice. This interaction underscores the ongoing debate within **Argentina** regarding economic policy and its social implications, with the **Catholic Church** advocating for policies that prioritize the welfare of vulnerable populations.

**South Africa's African National Congress (ANC)** has recorded its worst-ever performance in recent municipal elections. The party, historically associated with **Nelson Mandela**, saw its support decline, with voters reportedly blaming the **ANC** for persistent issues such as water and electricity shortages. This electoral outcome signals a significant shift in **South African** politics and reflects growing public dissatisfaction with the ruling party's governance.

## Regional Security and Environmental Concerns

In **Colombia**, the armed group **ELN (National Liberation Army)** has reportedly struck back against efforts by **US** forces, who are assisting **Colombia** and **Venezuela** in hunting the group. Officials reported that the **ELN** attacked a **Colombian** military base with drones over the weekend. This incident highlights the persistent challenge posed by armed groups in the region and the involvement of external actors in counter-insurgency efforts.

Meanwhile, **India** is celebrating Diwali, the Hindu festival of lights, with prayers, lights, and fireworks. However, concerns over air quality, particularly in **Delhi**, have led **India's** top court to decree that only more environmentally friendly fireworks could be used this weekend. Environmental activists have described this measure as a "greenwash," suggesting that it may not adequately address the severe air pollution issues faced by the city. This situation underscores the ongoing tension between cultural traditions and urgent environmental health concerns in rapidly developing urban centers.
