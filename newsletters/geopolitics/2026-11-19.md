Today's geopolitical landscape is marked by high-level diplomatic engagements in **Asia**, escalating regional tensions, and significant developments in international justice and security across the globe. A historic trilateral meeting between the leaders of the **United States**, **China**, and **Russia** is underway at the **APEC** summit in **Shenzhen**, while concerns about nuclear proliferation in **North Korea** and a crucial **International Court of Justice** ruling on the **Rohingya** genocide case against **Myanmar** also draw attention. In the **Americas**, new military agreements and shifts in anti-drug cooperation are reshaping regional dynamics, and a key conviction has been made in the assassination of **Haiti's** president. Meanwhile, the ongoing conflict in **Ukraine** saw another attack on the **Crimean Bridge**.

## Asia-Pacific Diplomacy and Tensions

A highly anticipated trilateral meeting involving **United States** President **Donald Trump**, **Chinese** leader **Xi Jinping**, and **Russian** President **Vladimir Putin** is taking place in **Shenzhen**, **China**, on the sidelines of the **Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC)** summit. **President Xi Jinping** greeted **President Trump** on the tarmac upon his arrival, and later shook hands with both **Trump** and **Putin** as leaders gathered. This marks the first three-way talks between the leaders of these global powers. Observers are closely watching the summit for potential discussions on trade, regional security, and global stability, with **India** also monitoring the developments. **China** and the **United States** are reportedly seeking to alter their economic bargain, moving from decoupling to "derisking".

Concurrently, **China** is reportedly engaged in a concerted campaign to portray **Japan** as an aggressor and a resurgent military threat, according to an analysis by the *New York Times*. This "war of words" indicates intensifying tensions between the two **Asian** powers.

In a separate development, **Rafael Grossi**, the head of the **International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)**, stated that **North Korea** has expanded its uranium enrichment plant. This ongoing expansion remains a "cause for serious concern," highlighting persistent challenges to nuclear non-proliferation efforts in the region.

Furthermore, the **International Court of Justice (ICJ)** is set to issue a ruling on the **Rohingya** genocide case against **Myanmar**. **The Gambia** initiated the case, accusing **Myanmar** of genocide following a 2017 military crackdown that led to the displacement of at least 730,000 **Rohingya** people. The ruling will address whether **Myanmar's** military operations in **Rakhine State** a decade ago were conducted with the intent to destroy the **Rohingya** minority, either in whole or in part.

## The Americas: Shifting Alliances and Justice

In **Haiti**, **Christian Emmanuel Sanon**, aged 67, has been found guilty in a **Miami** court for his role in the plot to assassinate **President Jovenel Moïse**. **Sanon** now faces the possibility of life in prison for the killing of the former **Haitian** leader. This conviction represents a significant step in the ongoing legal proceedings related to the 2021 assassination.

Meanwhile, **Colombia** has reportedly rejected a joint plan with the **United States** aimed at targeting drug gangs. **U.S.** officials indicated that the **Colombian** president stated his nation would not permit **American** forces to participate in anti-cartel military operations on **Colombian** soil. The **Colombian** government later denied that it had rejected a cooperation agreement. This development signals a potential shift in bilateral anti-narcotics strategies between the two countries.

In a notable diplomatic move, **Pete Hegseth** unveiled a new military deal between the **United States** and **Venezuela** during his first visit to the country since the **U.S.** capture of **Nicolas Maduro**. The specifics of this military agreement have not been fully detailed but mark a significant development in **U.S.-Venezuelan** relations following a period of considerable tension.

## European Security

The **Crimean Bridge**, a crucial link between **Russia** and the occupied territory of **Crimea**, has reportedly been attacked again. The head of **Crimea** stated that traffic on the bridge was suspended following the incident. This marks another instance of an attack on the strategic infrastructure amidst the ongoing conflict in **Ukraine**.
