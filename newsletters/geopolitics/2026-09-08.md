Global attention is currently drawn to several significant developments across different regions. Tensions in the Middle East have escalated, particularly around the **Strait of Hormuz**, impacting global energy markets. A trade dispute between **Canada** and the **United States** shows signs of intensifying with new tariffs. In Europe, **Germany** is grappling with the implications of a recent far-right electoral success, while diplomatic efforts continue regarding the conflict in **Ukraine**. Meanwhile, in Latin America, **Washington** is actively engaging with regional partners amidst a backdrop of shifting political dynamics and growing competition with **China**.

## Middle East: Heightened Tensions and Diplomatic Pressures

The geopolitical landscape in the Middle East remains highly volatile, with a notable increase in tensions in the **Strait of Hormuz**. Recent reports indicate that an average of only 10 ships transited the Strait daily over the past 10 days, the lowest level since May, according to Kpler data. This reduction in traffic coincides with intensified **US-Iran** strikes in the crucial waterway, leading to a surge in oil prices to six-week highs. The **United States** Central Command reported carrying out strikes on military targets in **Iran** following suspected **Iranian** attempts on ships in the **Strait of Hormuz** and **US** military bases. Specifically, the **US** military stated it struck two **Iranian** launchers preparing to fire rockets with naval mines. **Iran** has also claimed to strike an unmanned **US** vessel attempting to enter the **Strait of Hormuz**, a claim the **US** military dismissed as a "total lie". In retaliation for **Navy** warships being targeted with ballistic missiles, **US** forces struck three **Iranian** oil tankers. **Iran**, in turn, launched missiles at **US** bases in **Jordan** and the **United Arab Emirates**. **Qatar** has issued a stark warning of a potential "industrial catastrophe" if the crisis in the Strait continues.

In related developments, the **United Kingdom** is reportedly poised to announce a trade ban targeting **Israeli** West Bank settlements. Foreign Secretary **Ed Miliband** is expected to lay out the policy on Tuesday, which would cover imports, exports, and certain services including financing and advertising connected to settlement activity. The **UK** has long maintained that **Israeli** settlements are illegal under international law. This anticipated move aims to bring the **UK** into compliance with the International Court of Justice's advisory opinion of July 2024, which declared the **Israeli** occupation of Palestinian territory illegal. Prior to this, the **UK** had sanctioned entities and individuals linked to settler violence and issued strengthened business guidance discouraging economic engagement with settlements. Concurrently, **Nicaragua** has formally challenged **Germany's** arms exports to **Israel** at the International Court of Justice (**ICJ**). **Nicaragua** accuses **Germany** of violating the 1948 Genocide Convention and international humanitarian law by supplying weapons to **Israel** despite being aware of the risk that they could be used to commit serious violations in **Gaza**. **Germany**, as **Israel's** second-largest arms supplier, has requested the **ICJ** to dismiss the case on jurisdictional grounds, arguing that **Nicaragua** did not properly notify it and that the case inadvertently rules on a third party (**Israel**). **Germany** asserts that it has not authorized exports of weapons of war with a final destination in **Israel** that can be used in the **Gaza** conflict since 2024. Separately, **Houthi**-run media outlets in **Yemen** have accused **Saudi Arabia** of conducting an air strike on the al-Hazm prison, allegedly killing seven individuals.

## North America: Deepening Trade Frictions

A significant trade dispute between the **United States** and **Canada** is escalating, marked by retaliatory measures and threats of further action. **Canadian** counter-measures, covering several sectors, are set to impose tariffs ranging from 15% to 50% on approximately C$27.6 billion (around $20 billion **US**) worth of **American** imports, effective early on Tuesday. These tariffs are designed to match, dollar-for-dollar, the **US** duties on **Canadian** goods and will target sectors such as steel, dairy, appliances, agricultural equipment, pulp and paper, and electronics. This comes after **President Trump** announced 50% tariffs on a broad range of **Canadian** products, including dairy, alcoholic beverages, and some industrial equipment, which took effect on August 22, 2026. In a further escalation of rhetoric, **President Trump** has threatened to halt the sale of **Canadian Bombardier** jets in the **US**. This threat against one of **Canada's** largest companies comes amid the growing trade war between the two neighboring nations. **Washington** has also warned of a new round of **American** tariffs if **Canada** proceeds with its retaliatory measures, with **President Trump** previously suggesting increased auto tariffs. The dispute has been characterized by strong rhetoric, with a **US** Treasury Secretary reportedly comparing **Canada** to a "little yippy dog".

## Europe and Latin America: Political Shifts and External Engagements

In Europe, **Germany** is navigating a complex domestic political landscape following a recent state election that saw a significant victory for the far-right Alternative for Germany (**AfD**) party. The **AfD** won 43.8% of the vote in the **Saxony-Anhalt** state election on September 6, 2026, more than doubling its support from five years prior and marking its best result in any state election. This outcome has been described as a "political landslide" and an "electoral earthquake". The ruling Christian Democrats (**CDU**), led by **Chancellor Friedrich Merz**, saw their support plummet to 17.2%, losing about half their previous backing. While the **AfD** fell three seats short of an absolute majority, its success has initiated a re-evaluation of **Germany's** postwar strategy to safeguard democracy and has tested the nation's decades-old "firewall" against extremist parties. Parts of the **AfD**, including its **Saxony-Anhalt** chapter, are classified as "right-wing extremist" by **Germany's** domestic intelligence agency. **Chancellor Merz** acknowledged the "shock" victory, stating it was an election result that "we cannot simply move past".

Diplomatic efforts to address the conflict in **Ukraine** continue, with **US** envoys **Steve Witkoff** and **Jared Kushner** holding "encouraging" and "meaningful discussions" with **Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy** in **Kyiv**. These talks followed their meeting with **Russian President Vladimir Putin** in **Moscow**. **President Trump** had previously stated that the envoys were bringing "a proposal to end the war". Reports suggest that a proposed peace plan could require **Kyiv** to make significant concessions, potentially including withdrawal from parts of **Donbas**, arrangements over disputed territories, a commitment to remain outside **NATO**, and possible limits on the size of **Ukraine's** armed forces. Both **Russia** and **Ukraine** agreed to a three-day ceasefire on strikes during the period of these negotiations.

Across Latin America, a rightward political shift is evident, coinciding with increased engagement from **Washington**. **Senator Marco Rubio** is currently on a diplomatic tour, visiting **Ecuador**, **Colombia**, and **Peru**. This tour follows a wave of conservative electoral successes in the region since April 2025, with leaders such as **Peru's Keiko Fujimori** and **Colombia's Abelardo de la Espriella** taking office. **Rubio** has characterized this as an "extraordinary achievement" for **US** influence in the Western Hemisphere. The aim of his trip is to strengthen key **US** partnerships, increase cooperation on combating drug cartels, and deepen trade relations. However, the **Trump Administration's** efforts to counter **China's** growing economic ties in the region face limitations, as exemplified in **Peru**. Despite **Secretary of State Rubio's** visit, **Peru's** deepening economic relationship with **China** shows no signs of reversing course, leading to tensions with the **United States**. **China** is the leading economic partner for many Latin American countries, with significant investments in critical minerals and infrastructure, including the **Chinese**-built **Chancay** deep-water port in **Peru**. **Peru** has reportedly pushed back against **US** warnings regarding the port, and a free trade agreement with **China** is being optimized.
