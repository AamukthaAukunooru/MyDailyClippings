## Middle East Political Landscape

In **Israel**, the political scene is experiencing significant movement as opposition figures reportedly advance efforts to form a new government. **Gadi Eisenkot**, a former military chief and a prominent opposition leader, has reportedly set a deadline of December 6 for the formation of a new **Israeli** government. This development signals a concerted push to potentially unseat Prime Minister **Benjamin Netanyahu**. **Avigdor Liberman**, an ally of **Eisenkot**, has publicly stated that **Eisenkot** is currently on track to achieve this objective, indicating a serious challenge to the current leadership. The outcome of these efforts could lead to a substantial reconfiguration of **Israel's** political leadership and potentially influence its regional and international policies.

Meanwhile, in **Iraq**, a crucial ruling by the country's top court has granted the central government the authority to take control over **Kurdish** borders and oil fields. This decision comes just one day after **Kurdish** authorities voiced strong accusations, describing **Baghdad's** actions as an "assault" on the **Kurdish** region's constitutional rights. The ruling has significant implications for the autonomy of the **Kurdish** region and the distribution of vital oil revenues, potentially escalating long-standing tensions between the central government and the **Kurdish** regional administration over resource control and territorial sovereignty.

## International Diplomacy and Economic Shifts

**Japan** has taken a distinct stance regarding the **International Criminal Court (ICC)**, with its leader, **Sanae Takaichi**, publicly defending the institution and pledging to accelerate financial support. This position marks a notable divergence from the **United States**, particularly under the **Trump** administration, which has reportedly sought to weaken the global body. **Takaichi's** welcome of the **ICC's** president underscores **Japan's** commitment to international legal frameworks and multilateral institutions, even when it contrasts with the policies of a key ally.

A diplomatic incident has unfolded between **North Korea** and **Switzerland**, resulting in **North Korea's** expulsion of **Swiss** diplomats and the subsequent closure of **Switzerland's** office in **Pyongyang**. This escalation followed **Switzerland's** decision to impose sanctions on an international youth camp located in **North Korea**, which has been accused of hosting **Ukrainian** children for indoctrination purposes. The incident highlights the intricate web of international relations, with the **Ukraine** conflict having ripple effects on diplomatic ties and sanctions regimes globally.

In the realm of global trade, the **United States** has announced the implementation of new tariffs targeting a broad array of countries. These levies are set to affect many of **America's** largest trading partners, including **China** and the **European Union**. Such measures are likely to have widespread economic implications, potentially reshaping international trade flows and supply chains across various industries.

Concurrently, a significant economic transaction involves the sale of valuable foreign assets belonging to the **Russian** energy giant **Lukoil**. An **American**-led consortium has reportedly signed a deal to acquire these properties. Notably, Middle Eastern business contacts associated with peace negotiators **Steve Witkoff** and **Jared Kushner** are no longer involved in this particular transaction. The review underscores the complexities of international military partnerships and the scrutiny applied to operations involving foreign assistance and training.
