My Daily Clippings

The global geopolitical landscape remains highly dynamic, marked by escalating tensions in the **Middle East**, evolving alliances, and persistent security challenges. Recent developments include direct confrontations in critical maritime chokepoints, a significant shift in the **Yemen** conflict, and a deepening of security cooperation in **Latin America**. Meanwhile, **North Korea** continues to leverage its strategic partnerships, and the nature of the global terrorism threat, particularly from **Al-Qaeda** affiliates, continues to adapt.

## Middle East Tensions Escalate in Key Waterways

Tensions in the **Middle East** have seen a notable escalation, particularly in the **Strait of Hormuz** and the **Bab el-Mandeb Strait**. **Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC)** claimed on Thursday to have struck a **US** unmanned surveillance vessel, identified as a "Sildrone" with hull number 5838, in the **Strait of Hormuz**. **Iran** asserted that the strait remains under its "intelligence surveillance and smart control," warning that any hostile movement would be targeted. The **US** military has not yet confirmed the strike or its extent. This incident follows earlier claims by **Iran's Revolutionary Guards** of capturing an advanced **US** unmanned underwater vehicle, an **Anduril Dive-LD**, near the entrance to the **Strait of Hormuz**. The **Pentagon** responded by stating that a **US** underwater drone had "malfunctioned more than a day ago" and was an older model without sensitive data.

Concurrently, the conflict in **Yemen** has entered what the **UN** Special Envoy for **Yemen**, **Hans Grundberg**, described as a "new and more dangerous phase". The **Iran**-backed **Houthi** rebels reportedly seized the strategic Red Sea port city of **Mokha** on Thursday, marking a significant territorial gain. This advance positions the **Houthis** closer to exerting control over the **Bab el-Mandeb Strait**, a critical maritime chokepoint connecting the Red Sea and the Gulf of Aden, through which approximately 12% of global goods typically pass. Analysts suggest that **Houthi** control over this waterway could provide **Iran** with additional leverage against the **United States** and its allies, especially given existing disruptions to shipping in the **Strait of Hormuz**. The **UN** envoy expressed serious concerns about the threat to freedom of navigation posed by these developments.

Further regional instability was highlighted by **Israel's** announcement on Thursday that it had destroyed **Hezbollah's** underground infrastructure at the **Ali al-Taher** ridge in southern **Lebanon**. **Israeli** Prime Minister **Benjamin Netanyahu** and Defense Minister **Israel Katz** stated that this operation completed the establishment of a security zone in the area. They characterized the demolished infrastructure, reportedly built over two decades with **Iranian** financing, as a staging base intended for an invasion of the **Galilee**. The demolition was reported to have caused a 4.1-magnitude earthquake.

## Evolving Alliances and Global Security Posture

The **Trump** administration announced that **Peru** will join the "**Shield of the Americas**" security alliance, a **US**-led initiative aimed at enhancing military operations and intelligence sharing against drug cartels in **Latin America**. **Peru's** recently elected right-wing leader, **Keiko Fujimori**, confirmed the country's participation after discussions with **US** Secretary of State **Marco Rubio**. This move signifies a more militarized approach by the **United States** to regional security in **Latin America**, a strategy that has previously seen the **Trump** administration express concerns over **Peru's** economic ties with **China**.

In **East Asia**, **South Korea** is navigating increasing pressure from the **Trump** administration regarding its involvement in securing the **Strait of Hormuz**. **President Trump** has publicly criticized **Seoul's** reluctance to commit naval forces to assist **US** operations against **Iran**, even leading to a reduction in joint military exercises. While **South Korea** is reportedly considering deploying a naval support ship and patrol aircraft, officials have emphasized the need for parliamentary approval and a careful assessment to ensure national defense capabilities are not compromised. Adding to the complexity, **Iran** has issued warnings that **South Korea's** participation in military operations in the **Strait of Hormuz** would result in "grave consequences".

Meanwhile, **North Korea** appears to be benefiting from its deepening alliance with **Moscow** and its support for the war in **Ukraine**. This partnership is reportedly providing **Pyongyang** with an economic boost, enabling the regime to pursue various long-desired arms and infrastructure projects. Reports indicate that **North Korea** is supplying ammunition and potentially troops to **Russia** in exchange for economic and military assistance. A new road bridge connecting **North Korea** and **Russia** across the **Tumen River** has also recently opened, intended to facilitate increased trade and exchange between the two nations.

## Evolving Terrorism Threat 25 Years After 9/11

Twenty-five years after the September 11 attacks, the global terrorism landscape has significantly evolved. **Al-Qaeda**, while no longer operating from a centralized geographical hub, has transformed into a network of diverse affiliates and splinter groups active across **Africa**, the **Middle East**, and **South Asia**. Experts note that despite the weakening of its core leadership, these regional offshoots maintain significant strength and continue to inspire attacks. The nature of the threat is now more geographically dispersed, with groups such as **Al-Shabaab** in **Somalia** and **Jama'at Nusrat al-Islam wal-Muslimin (JNIM)** in the **Sahel** demonstrating substantial operational capabilities. These groups often engage in localized violence, including stabbings and vehicle rammings, reflecting an adaptive approach to maintaining their presence and influence.
