The global geopolitical landscape continues to be shaped by ongoing conflicts in the Middle East, intensifying strategic competition between major powers, and evolving security dynamics across Europe and the Americas. Diplomatic efforts are underway in various hotspots, while economic strategies are being recalibrated in response to shifting alliances and internal challenges.

## Middle East Conflicts and US Policy

The **United States** is reportedly planning to resume deliveries of 2,000-lb bombs to **Israel**, a move that follows a two-year halt on such shipments due to concerns over mass casualties during **Israel’s** bombing of **Gaza**. This development comes as **Israeli** strikes in **Gaza** continue, with a recent incident resulting in the deaths of a teenager and a civil defense worker in a residential neighborhood, according to civil defense officials. **Israel** is currently facing international pressure to curtail its attacks.

Meanwhile, the conflict in **Yemen** remains fierce, with **Houthis** reporting deadly **Saudi** airstrikes and **Saudi Arabia** issuing mass alerts to its populace. **Saudi Arabia** is reportedly facing growing security and economic challenges as the **Houthis** gain ground in **Yemen**, necessitating a delicate balance between deterrence and diplomacy in the escalating conflict. In a related diplomatic push, **Iran’s** top diplomat has traveled to **China**, as **Beijing** seeks to mediate an end to the ongoing conflict.

The protracted nature of these conflicts has had implications for **US** military readiness. A **Pentagon** inspector has confirmed that the **Iran** war has led to shortfalls in **US** munitions, also noting a bottleneck in resupply efforts, despite **President Donald Trump's** assertions of "virtually limitless" **US** supplies. Against this backdrop, **Republican Representative Thomas Massie** has called for the impeachment of **US Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth**, emerging as an outspoken critic of the war on **Iran** and broader overseas military interventions.

## US-China Strategic Competition and Global Influence

The strategic rivalry between the **United States** and **China** continues to manifest across various domains, including technology and regional influence. **President Trump** and **President Xi Jinping** reportedly hold fundamentally different ideas regarding artificial intelligence technology and its regulation, suggesting a lack of common ground on AI safety. This technological competition also raises questions about data security, with concerns surfacing regarding whether **Chinese** firms are inadvertently or intentionally providing data to the **United States**, potentially highlighting a weakness for **Beijing** in the AI competition.

The competition extends to **Latin America**, where **President Trump**, in his returned presidency, has expressed renewed determination to mitigate **Chinese** influence, particularly in the Western Hemisphere. An example of this is **Mexico's** deepening technology cooperation with **Beijing** in sectors that are sensitive to **US** security concerns. The broader implications of **US** foreign policy in the **Asia-Pacific** are also under scrutiny, as a recent suggestion by a think tank for a **US** pullback from the western Pacific has reportedly alarmed **US** allies in the region, with analysts warning such a move could weaken deterrence and strengthen **China's** position. The delicate situation in the **Taiwan Strait** underscores the need for sustained dialogue, as a lack of communication between **Beijing** and **Washington** could leave both vulnerable to dangerous misreadings of each other’s intentions.

## North American Diplomacy and European Security Concerns

**Canada** is actively pursuing economic diplomacy, with **Prime Minister Mark Carney** leading efforts to attract global investment. He recently convened approximately 100 money managers in **Toronto**, pitching **Canada** as a stable haven for investment in a turbulent global environment. The inaugural **Canada** Investment Summit highlighted a $1 trillion sales pitch and over 160 projects on offer, with a notable pivot towards the **European Union** amidst an ongoing trade dispute with the **United States**.

In **Europe**, security concerns persist, with **NATO** reportedly suspecting **Russia** of being behind recent drone incursions in **Lithuania**, leading to questions about whether such airspace violations are becoming a "new normal" for the continent. Energy politics also remain a point of contention between **Russia** and **Ukraine**. Despite claims from **President Trump** about an energy truce between the two nations, both **Ukraine** and **Russia** have denied any such agreement, with the **Kremlin** stating that while an agreement would be a "very good idea," it would likely have little effect on fuel markets. Further east, **Japan** faces difficult decisions regarding its energy security, as it seeks to reduce its reliance on **Russian** energy without further antagonizing **Russia** by cutting liquefied natural gas (LNG) purchases.

Separately, **Argentina** is intensifying its campaign against oil companies operating near the **Falkland Islands**, with plans to file new legal complaints against firms exploring for oil in the vicinity.
