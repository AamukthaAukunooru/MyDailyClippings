The global geopolitical landscape continues to exhibit a complex interplay of emerging power blocs, escalating regional conflicts, and persistent domestic challenges. This week, the **BRICS** summit in **New Delhi** highlights the growing influence of non-Western powers, even as internal disagreements persist. Simultaneously, the Middle East is experiencing a significant escalation, particularly in the **Red Sea** region, with **Houthi** advances in **Yemen** and attacks on **Saudi Arabian** infrastructure. Tensions also remain elevated in the Indo-Pacific, with ongoing discussions surrounding the **Australia-US** alliance in the context of **China's** rise.

## Global Blocs and Shifting Alliances

The leaders of the **BRICS** club, including **China**, **Russia**, **India**, and **Iran**, are convening in **New Delhi** this weekend, underscoring the expanding influence of this economic and political grouping. Despite the growing membership, the bloc is reportedly divided by various issues, including ongoing conflicts and soaring energy prices. The expansion of **BRICS** continues to attract emerging powers, reflecting a desire among some nations for alternatives to Western-led institutions. Within the bloc, competition for leadership and agenda-setting remains apparent, particularly between **India** and **China**. **India's** foreign policy approach, described as "America Plus," seeks to deepen ties with **China** and **Russia** while simultaneously preventing **BRICS** from becoming an exclusively anti-Western platform. This strategy aims to balance relationships with both established Western powers and the burgeoning **BRICS** bloc.

In the Indo-Pacific, the alliance between **Australia** and the **United States** is under scrutiny, with some experts warning that **Australia** risks being unwillingly drawn into a conflict with **China**. Concerns have been raised regarding **Australia's** increasing enmeshment in the **US** military apparatus. Former **Australian** Prime Minister **Kevin Rudd** has emphasized the importance of physical security in this evolving geopolitical environment. Meanwhile, **North Korea** continues to solidify its alliances with **Russia** and **China**, accelerating its nuclear program. Discussions surrounding denuclearization in dialogue between **North Korea** and the **United States** remain complex, with differing perspectives from both sides. **Pyongyang's** leader, **Kim Jong Un**, reportedly sees no need for a deal with the **US**, given the current geopolitical alignments.

## Escalation in the Middle East

The conflict in **Yemen** has intensified significantly, with **Houthi** forces reportedly seizing control of parts of **Yemen's Red Sea** coast, including the strategic island of Perim in the **Bab al-Mandab Strait**. This advance by the **Iran**-backed **Houthis** represents a notable military gain for the group in recent years. The **Bab al-Mandab Strait** is a critical choke point for global trade, particularly for oil shipments, and its control by the **Houthis** poses a new threat to international shipping routes. Alongside these territorial gains, **Saudi Arabia** has announced the suspension of its crucial East-West oil pipeline following a drone attack, which it attributes to drones launched from **Iraq**. **Iraq** has condemned the attack and dismissed a military commander in the Maysan province, from where the drones were allegedly launched. This escalation is seen by some analysts as a dangerous new dimension to the broader **Iran** war, with **Tehran** pursuing a cautious escalation strategy ahead of **US** midterm elections.

Further regional tensions are evident in **Lebanon**, where **Israel** has reportedly destroyed an underground **Hezbollah** base in the southern part of the country. The complex was located below the **Ali al-Taher** ridge, a strategic hilltop overlooking the region, which has been a site of intense fighting.

## Americas and Africa Developments

In **Colombia**, the government is set to resume aerial spraying of coca crops, a move that has quickly been challenged by a local court due to health concerns. This decision follows discussions between **Colombia's** president and **US** Secretary of State **Marco Rubio** regarding enhanced measures against drug trafficking.

On the African continent, the **United Nations** has extended a partial arms embargo on **Sudan** for an additional month. **Sudan's UN** ambassador has argued that a total arms embargo would infringe upon the country's right to self-defense, as guaranteed by the **UN Charter**.
