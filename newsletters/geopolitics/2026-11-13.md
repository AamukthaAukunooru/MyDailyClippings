This past forty-eight hours has seen significant developments across global technology, international diplomacy, and **US** foreign policy. The resignation of **Nvidia** CEO **Jensen Huang** has sent ripples through the artificial intelligence sector, while diplomatic efforts to address the **Russia-Ukraine** conflict are intensifying with the inclusion of **Europe** in peace talks. Meanwhile, **US President Donald Trump** has made notable statements regarding **Cuba** and **Iran**, and the **AUKUS** defense pact is facing renewed scrutiny.

## US Politics and Tech Geopolitics

**Jensen Huang**, the chief executive officer of **Nvidia**, a leading artificial intelligence chipmaking company, has resigned from his position. This departure follows revelations of alleged extramarital affairs, including one reportedly involving a suspected **Chinese** spy. **US** lawmakers have expressed their intent to question **Huang** regarding this alleged relationship. The resignation has created significant disruption within the artificial intelligence industry. Furthermore, concerns have been raised regarding **Nvidia's** sales of chips to companies such as **Megaspeed**. Critics suggest that **US** Commerce Secretary **Howard Lutnick** and his department have adopted a lenient stance towards **Nvidia** despite increasing calls for stricter controls on chip exports to **China**.

In **US** domestic politics, **Senator Chris Van Hollen**, a Democrat representing **Maryland**, announced his intention to challenge **Senator Chuck Schumer** for the role of **Senate Majority Leader**. This challenge comes in the wake of recent sweeping midterm election victories for the Democratic party. **Senator Van Hollen** stated that "The American people called for change," indicating a desire for new leadership within the party.

## Ukraine Conflict and Diplomacy

In the **Baltic Sea** region, **Finland** has reported detecting a strike on a **Russian** warship. This incident was reported prior to meetings between **US** envoys and the presidents of both **Russia** and **Ukraine**.

Diplomatic efforts aimed at resolving the **Russia-Ukraine** conflict are gaining momentum. **Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy** has welcomed the **United States'** initiative to bring **Europe** into the ongoing peace talks. **President Zelenskyy** underscored the importance of **European** leaders' participation, especially given **Ukraine's** aspirations to eventually join the **European Union**. Reports indicate that **US** envoys, including **Witkoff** and **Kushner**, held discussions with **Russian President Vladimir Putin** in **Moscow**, where the inclusion of **Europe** in future peace negotiations was a key topic. While **Ukraine** has confirmed that **European** representatives would join the next round of talks, **Russia** has not yet officially corroborated this development. **European Union** leaders are reportedly engaged in discussions to determine who will represent **Europe** in these crucial talks, following recent comments from a **US** envoy who met with **President Putin**. The first trilateral talks in nine months, involving **Ukraine**, the **United States**, and **Russia**, recently concluded in **Abu Dhabi**. These discussions resulted in an agreement on exchanging prisoners, which is seen as a potential step forward in diplomatic efforts.

## US Foreign Policy and Alliances

**US President Donald Trump** has indicated a potential move to topple authorities in **Cuba** before the end of his presidential term, describing **Cuba** as a "failed nation." This statement suggests a possible significant shift in **US** policy towards the Caribbean island nation. Separately, **President Trump** also disclosed that **Gulf** allies had requested him to postpone further strikes against **Iran**. This highlights ongoing consultations and the influence of regional partners on **US** military actions in the **Middle East**.

In the **Indo-Pacific** region, the **AUKUS** security pact, involving **Australia**, the **United Kingdom**, and the **United States**, is facing criticism. **Bob Carr**, a former **Australian Labor** foreign affairs minister, asserted that the **AUKUS** deal is "living a lie." **Carr** contended that **Australia** would "never get nuclear submarines from the **US**" and that both signatory nations are aware of this reality. He further characterized the three hundred sixty-eight billion dollar deal as having been reduced to a "diplomatic joke."
