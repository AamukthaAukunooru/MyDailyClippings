The global geopolitical landscape continues to be shaped by a confluence of regional conflicts, economic uncertainties, and evolving security threats. Tensions in the Middle East have escalated with renewed Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabian infrastructure, while international financial bodies issue stark warnings about the resilience of the global economy amidst ongoing crises. Meanwhile, concerns over hybrid warfare and cybersecurity are rising following a significant cyberattack in France and revelations about alleged sabotage in Europe. Diplomatic relations are also being tested, notably between South Korea and Ukraine, as the conflict in Eastern Europe continues to draw international attention and intervention.

## Middle East Tensions and Global Economic Outlook

Recent days have seen a significant escalation of hostilities in the Middle East, with **Yemen's Houthi** movement claiming multiple missile and drone attacks on **Saudi Arabia**. The **Saudi** capital, **Riyadh**, has been targeted for three consecutive days, with reports of ballistic missiles aimed at **King Khalid International Airport** and another facility, resulting in 13 fatalities. **Saudi** officials have vowed a "serious response" to these attacks. **US President Donald Trump** condemned the **Houthis**, stating they were "misbehaving," and flights at **Riyadh** airport were reportedly halted. These developments occur amid broader scrutiny of regional alignments, including the **United Arab Emirates'** backing of **Ethiopia** and **Saudi Arabia's** diplomatic role in the **Tigray** conflict. Further raising concerns, the **United Kingdom** has indicated that **Iran** is "highly likely" to be behind an incident at **RAF Fairford** that led to the withdrawal of **USAF B-1** heavy bombers, prompting the **UK** government to summon **Iran's** ambassador.

These escalating regional tensions are casting a shadow over the global economic outlook. The **International Monetary Fund (IMF)** has warned that the global economy "could soon test the limits of resilience," citing low oil stocks and overstretched national budgets. The **IMF's World Economic Outlook** also projected that a worsening **Middle East** crisis could lead to a significant increase in fuel prices, potentially by 30 cents a litre across **Australia**, impacting global growth and inflation.

## Hybrid Warfare and Cybersecurity Threats

The realm of hybrid warfare and cybersecurity has also seen notable developments. **France** experienced an "unprecedented" cyberattack targeting its supermarkets, leading to widespread food losses, with hackers reportedly raising temperatures in supermarket fridges, causing fires and spoiling food in a "large-scale" incident. This incident underscores the growing vulnerability of critical infrastructure to sophisticated digital attacks.

In response to intensifying sabotage, arson, and cyberattacks by **Russia** against **European** nations supporting **Ukraine**, the **North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO)** has detailed a plan to shield **Ukraine's European allies**. This blueprint outlines measures to deter **Moscow** in the event of a broader conflict. Separately, **Polish** authorities have reported the arrests of two **Turkish** citizens in **Germany** in connection with an alleged act of sabotage involving a **Starlink** station fire last month. **Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk** characterized the incident as an act of "hybrid warfare," highlighting the evolving nature of international security threats.

## Geopolitical Shifts and Diplomatic Frictions

The broader geopolitical landscape continues to be marked by ongoing conflicts and diplomatic strains. In **Sudan**, dozens of people were reportedly killed in airstrikes near the **Sudan-Egypt** border. These attacks occurred at a remote gold mine, hundreds of miles from the main front lines of **Sudan's** civil war, with videos suggesting the use of **Egyptian** munitions. This incident points to the potential for the conflict to draw in regional actors and expand geographically.

The conflict in **Ukraine** remains a focal point, with **Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky** stating that six **Ukrainian** energy sites have been hit by **Russia** since **US President Trump** announced a ceasefire. **Zelensky** noted that despite the **US** president's announcement that energy infrastructure would not be targeted, strikes have continued. This follows **President Trump's** decision to suspend sanctions on **Russian** diesel fuel, a move that reportedly "stunned **Ukrainian** and **European** officials".

Adding to the complex diplomatic environment, a spat has emerged between **South Korea** and **Ukraine**. **South Korea** recalled its ambassador and demanded a public apology from **Ukraine** following a **North Korean** prisoner transfer that **Kyiv** reportedly revealed against **Seoul's** wishes. **South Korea** insists the transfer of two **North Korean** prisoners of war was intended to be kept secret to avoid antagonizing **Pyongyang**, leading to accusations and counter-accusations between the two nations.
