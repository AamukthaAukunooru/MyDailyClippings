My Daily Clippings

The global geopolitical landscape continues to present a complex array of challenges and shifts. In North America, domestic political tensions in the **United States** are unfolding alongside a significant trade dispute with **Canada**. The Middle East remains a focal point of escalating hostilities between the **United States** and **Iran**, while **Tunisia** grapples with renewed internal unrest. Meanwhile, economic realignments are visible in Asia, with **Japan** increasingly prioritizing investments in **India** over **China**, and the human cost of global conflicts is highlighted by reports of **Cameroonian** citizens being recruited to fight in **Ukraine**.

## North America & US Domestic Affairs

Internal political dynamics within the **United States** have seen the resignation of **Army Secretary Dan Driscoll**. **Driscoll**, the youngest-ever army secretary, served for approximately 18 months and reportedly had a strained relationship with **Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth**. White House spokesperson **Anna Kelly** stated that **Driscoll** had been "highly effective" in advancing **President Trump's** agenda and was involved in negotiations between **Russia** and **Ukraine**. His departure adds to a series of senior military officials who have left their posts since **President Trump** returned to the White House in January 2025.

Concurrently, the **US Supreme Court** delivered a 5-4 decision allowing **President Donald Trump's** proposed White House ballroom and military complex construction to continue indefinitely. The ruling blocked lower court decisions that would have halted above-ground construction, though the Supreme Court did not definitively rule on whether the President has unilateral authority for such a project without congressional approval. The **National Trust for Historic Preservation** had challenged the project, citing potential aesthetic, cultural, and historical harm, arguing that **Trump** lacked the authority as a "temporary tenant". However, the majority concluded that the trust was unlikely to establish the legal standing required to bring the lawsuit. **Chief Justice John Roberts** dissented, stating that the construction was "likely unlawful". The White House maintains the $400 million project is 65% complete, privately funded, and will be finished by summer 2028.

In a developing trade dispute, **Canada** has announced retaliatory tariffs against the **United States**, escalating tensions between the two allies. **Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney** has opposed **President Trump's** tariff policies, which include a 50% tariff on various Canadian goods. **Canada's** counter-tariffs, set to take effect on September 8, 2026, will apply to approximately $27.6 billion worth of US imports, matching US tariff rates product by product, ranging from 15% to 50%. **Carney** has accused **Washington** of attempting to "subordinate" **Canada** and "destroy our major industries," asserting that **Canada** will respond with resilience and a plan to protect its workers and businesses. The trade conflict has been ongoing, with previous negotiations collapsing and concerns raised about increased consumer prices in the **US** and negative impacts on small businesses.

## Middle East & North Africa

Hostilities between the **United States** and **Iran** have reignited, with **President Donald Trump** vowing to strike **Iran** "hard" after recent clashes. The escalation follows US strikes on **Larak Island** in the Strait of Hormuz, targeting what US Central Command described as **Iranian** minelaying forces posing an "imminent threat" to shipping. In retaliation, **Iran** reportedly fired at least eight ballistic missiles at US bases in **Jordan**. Senior US military officials have reportedly warned **Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth** that a prolonged, large-scale conflict risks draining US military resources and weakening its ability to confront other adversaries.

**Jordan** finds itself in a precarious position amidst the renewed **US-Iran** conflict. The kingdom has been caught between belligerents in a war it sees as not its own, with missiles and drones crossing its airspace and some strikes hitting facilities used by the US military within its borders. Attacks on American forces stationed in **Jordan**, including reported casualties, have reinforced concerns that hosting US troops is turning **Jordan** into a secondary battlefield. Over 200 Jordanian political, cultural, and military figures issued an open letter calling for the withdrawal of foreign forces and a review of the security agreement with the **United States**. While **Jordanian** policymakers have historically viewed **Iran** as a malign influence, many **Jordanians** also perceive the current confrontation as a war by and for **Israel**, deepening anti-American sentiment. The **Gulf Cooperation Council** has condemned **Iran's** attacks on **Jordan**, viewing them as a direct threat to regional security.

In North Africa, **Tunisia** is experiencing a resurgence of old grievances five years after **President Kais Saied's** power grab. Economic discontent persists, with widespread protests over water cuts, electricity outages, rising prices, and medicine shortages. While many Tunisians initially welcomed **Saied's** moves in 2021, hoping to end political paralysis and corruption, the promised improvements have largely failed to materialize. The space for dissent has significantly narrowed, and human rights groups accuse **Saied's** government of cracking down on opposition leaders, civil society groups, journalists, and activists. Protests in **Tunis** and other areas are increasingly rejecting "one-man rule" and demanding the restoration of democracy.

## Asia & Global Impact

**Japanese** companies are significantly increasing their investments in **India**, signaling a strategic pivot driven by deepening risks in **China** and a shrinking domestic market. **Japan** has pledged a substantial $68 billion in investment into **India** over the next decade, more than doubling its previous target. This comes as **Japanese** investment in **China** has reportedly collapsed by 70% in four years, while investment in **India** has surged sevenfold. The shift is backed by **Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi**, known for her hawkish stance on **China**, and includes significant investments in Indian auto manufacturing, semiconductor plants, and other critical sectors. This economic reorientation is viewed as a geopolitical move, strengthening the **Indo-Pacific** partnership between **Japan** and **India** against the backdrop of **Chinese** expansionism.

The human cost and global reach of the **Russia-Ukraine war** continue to emerge, with reports of **Cameroonian** citizens being recruited to fight for **Russia**. Driven by economic desperation and false promises of jobs or fast-tracked Russian citizenship, young men from **Cameroon** and other African nations are reportedly finding themselves on the front lines of the conflict. Ukrainian officials estimate that nearly 3,000 citizens from various African countries have fought or are fighting for **Russia**. **Cameroon's** government confirmed in April that **Russia** had reported the deaths of 16 Cameroonian nationals serving in its military in **Ukraine**, though independent monitors suggest the actual death toll may be much higher. Civil society activists in **Cameroon** have formed groups to document these cases and press authorities for information, highlighting concerns about recruitment networks and the repatriation of remains.
