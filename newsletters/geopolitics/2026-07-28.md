## Middle East Diplomacy and Conflicts

Diplomatic efforts regarding **Iran** have seen a pause in **United States** strikes, with **President Trump** indicating that talks are underway to potentially de-escalate the conflict. **Trump** stated that he had paused **US** strikes to allow for diplomacy, expressing optimism that "something could happen". However, **Iran** has denied that direct talks are currently taking place. This pause comes amid reports suggesting concerns about the **US's** missile reserves following extensive use in the conflict. **President Trump** has also voiced support for his defense secretary, **Pete Hegseth**, noting his performance has been "a great job".

Meanwhile, **Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu** is scheduled to meet with **President Trump** in **Washington**, navigating a delicate balance between discussions on **Iran** and upcoming elections in **Israel**. This meeting occurs as the **United Nations** has criticized **Israel's** continued expansion of settlements in the occupied **West Bank**, with **UN** chief **António Guterres** labeling such actions a "flagrant violation" of international law amidst escalating violence. Separately, the **Iranian**-backed **Houthi** militia in **Yemen** has reportedly exploited a vulnerable moment for **Saudi Arabia**, aiming to secure political concessions in **Yemen**. The **Trump** administration's proposed nuclear deal with **Saudi Arabia** has also drawn criticism, with some analysts arguing it could undermine decades of **US** counterproliferation policy.

## European Environmental and Diplomatic Strains

**Europe** is facing an "unprecedented" wildfire season, with **France** and **Spain** particularly affected. Firefighters are racing to contain large blazes before a new heatwave is expected to worsen conditions. Hundreds of thousands have been evacuated in central **Spain** and southwest **France** due to the crisis. The mayor of **Bordeaux**, **Thomas Cazenave**, has warned that a wildfire is now nine miles from the city, with preparations underway for potential further evacuations. The coming days are considered "absolutely critical" for firefighters as temperatures are projected to rise again this week.

In a separate diplomatic development, **US** diplomats staged a walkout during **France's** address to the **UN Security Council**. This incident occurred amidst tensions surrounding the reappointment of a human rights chief. This public display of disagreement highlights ongoing strains in relations between the two long-standing allies. Furthermore, a top **European Union** defense official has emphasized the need for **Europe** to learn from **Ukraine's** example in defense matters, stating, "We were looking at how to support **Ukraine**—now we are looking at how **Ukraine** can support us".

## Asia-Pacific and International Law Developments

In **India**, the youth-led "Cockroach" protest movement has dispersed after achieving some of its demands, raising broader questions about the country's education system and the future of dissent. The movement, driven by grievances over an exam scandal and social media satire, resulted in a shake-up within the **Indian** government, including the resignation of the education minister. This outcome is seen by some as a significant victory for **India's** young people, demonstrating their political influence.

Maritime incidents in the **South China Sea** continue to draw attention, with fifteen sailors still missing after a **Vietnamese** cargo ship sank in the heavily disputed waters. **China** reported rescuing 47 individuals who were aboard the vessel. This event underscores the inherent risks and ongoing activity in a region marked by competing territorial claims.

In **Africa**, **Chad's** government announced its intention to withdraw from the **International Criminal Court (ICC)**. The foreign ministry cited the court's "limited and uneven" effectiveness as a reason for its decision. This move follows accusations of repression against **Chad's** military leader. Meanwhile, **Peru's** new president, **Keiko Fujimori**, has taken office, promising enhanced security and investment. Her administration faces the challenge of navigating **Peru's** position amidst the geopolitical rivalry between **Washington** and **Beijing**.
