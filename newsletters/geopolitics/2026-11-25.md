## Eastern Europe and Beyond: The Ukraine Conflict's Evolving Landscape

The conflict in **Ukraine** has seen a notable escalation, with **Russian President Vladimir Putin** stating that retaliation is "inevitable" following recent **Ukrainian** attacks on **Russian** oil plants. These attacks have intensified after a mid-October declaration of an "energy ceasefire" by **US President Donald Trump**, which **Ukraine** reportedly resumed striking after. **Ukraine** has conducted 18 attacks on 16 **Russian** oil refineries since October 30, with two facilities struck twice. These strikes have reportedly reduced **Russia's** daily crude oil processing by approximately 45 percent since late October, from over 700,000 tonnes to around 400,000 tonnes.

In response to **Ukraine's** ongoing financial and military needs, the **European Union** is poised to finalize an additional 45 billion euros in aid for **Kyiv**. This proposed loan, intended to address **Ukraine's** estimated 70 billion euro financing gap for 2027, would be repaid only after **Russia** pays reparations for war damages. Up to 25 billion euros of this support is allocated for military assistance, with 20 billion euros for general budget support. This move by **Brussels** comes as **Washington's** direct financial support for **Ukraine** has reportedly retreated.

Concerns are also rising about the potential expansion of the conflict, with **Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy** alleging that two **Russian** vessels were targeted in **Libyan** waters, raising questions about **Libya** becoming a new front in the **Ukraine** war. Meanwhile, **Ukraine's** bid for **NATO** membership continues to face controversy, with a lack of unanimity among member states hindering rapid acceptance, as highlighted during the **NATO** Parliamentary Assembly in **Berlin**.

## Middle East: Escalating Tensions and Humanitarian Concerns

The **Middle East** continues to experience significant instability. In **Saudi Arabia**, a **Houthi** attack on a skyscraper in the capital, **Riyadh**, wounded 11 people. This incident marks the second strike on the **Public Investment Fund Tower** in less than a week, with the first causing material damage but no injuries. The **Iran-backed Houthi** militia and **Saudi Arabia** have been engaged in escalating exchanges of attacks for several weeks.

Tensions in **Israel** and **Gaza** have also intensified. **Israeli** police have reportedly confiscated mosque loudspeakers as far-right lawmakers push legislation to restrict the call to prayer, leading to accusations from **Israel's Ben-Gvir** of seeking a "religious war." Concurrently, an **Israeli** air strike in **Gaza** killed **Naeem Abu Nahel**, the director of **Hamas's** internal security apparatus, along with six other Palestinians. **Abu Nahel**, 50 years old, had held the position for only two months.

Separately, **Iran** is facing a severe humanitarian crisis as its medicine supply chain is reportedly failing. Pharmacies are closing, hospital deliveries have stalled, and patients are rationing doses or resorting to black markets.

The saga highlights the increasing importance of technology companies in global economic competition and national security.
