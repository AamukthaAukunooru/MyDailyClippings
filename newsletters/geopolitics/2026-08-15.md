## Middle East Tensions and US Foreign Policy Shifts

Tensions in the **Middle East** have been heightened by statements from **US President Donald Trump** concerning the record-setting deployment of the **USS Abraham Lincoln** aircraft carrier and the strategic **Strait of Hormuz**. The **USS Abraham Lincoln** has reportedly been at sea for a record 250 days, with **President Trump** dismissing concerns about the mental health of sailors, stating the deployment was "not nearly long enough". Despite reports of family members raising concerns and members of **Congress** calling for an investigation into conditions, the **President** confirmed that the **USS George Washington** would replace the carrier on its mission in the region. The typical deployment length for a US Navy aircraft carrier is around six to seven months. The extended deployment of the **USS Abraham Lincoln** has surpassed previous modern records, prompting calls for attention to the well-being of the crew.

Further escalating regional rhetoric, **President Trump** has also threatened to declare the **Strait of Hormuz** a "territory of the United States". This remark, made amid ongoing ceasefire negotiations between the **US** and **Iran**, has significant implications, as control over the strait remains a contentious point in the conflict. The **Strait of Hormuz** is an internationally recognized strait, crucial for global oil shipments. International law generally dictates that straits used for international navigation cannot be unilaterally claimed as sovereign territory, allowing for transit passage by all nations. The seriousness of **President Trump's** statement as a new policy position remains unclear.

## Europe's Climate Crisis and Regional Instability

**Europe** is currently experiencing a severe climate crisis, with record heatwaves fueling widespread wildfires and causing significant disruption. Wildfires have ravaged approximately 500,000 hectares across the continent, forcing thousands to evacuate. In **Croatia**, a major blaze near the port town of **Omis** has been described by officials as one of the worst in the country's history, injuring dozens and necessitating thousands of evacuations. **Italy** has also been affected, with the eruption of **Mount Etna** and subsequent volcanic ash leading to the closure of **Sicily's** busiest airport, suspending flights until at least Saturday afternoon and stranding hundreds of travelers. The **United Kingdom** is also facing drought conditions, prompting **Prime Minister Andy Burnham** to announce £65 million in aid for farmers, including measures for improved water access and support for building farm reservoirs, acknowledging that farmers should not bear the risks of the climate crisis alone.

In **Eastern Europe**, concerns about **Russian** influence persist following reports that **Moscow** actively campaigned to destabilize **Moldova's** pro-Western government. This interference reportedly involved various tactics, including paying **Orthodox** priests, implementing a vote-buying scheme, and establishing training camps focused on election interference. These efforts highlight ongoing geopolitical competition in the region as **Moldova** seeks closer ties with Western nations.

## Shifting Dynamics in the Israeli-Palestinian Conflict and Asia

The **Israeli-Palestinian** conflict continues to see significant unrest, particularly in the **West Bank**. **Israeli** soldiers have been accused of supporting "terrorist" settlers in **Qusra**, where two **Palestinian** families were reportedly besieged in their homes by violent settlers for several days. Activists have attempted to deliver aid to these trapped families. In response to such incidents, **Israeli Defense Minister Katz** has called for the military to transfer the responsibility for protecting **Palestinians** to the police, following the besieging of the two **West Bank** families.

Meanwhile, in **Southeast Asia**, a recent naval exercise involving **China** and **Indonesia** conducted east of **Taiwan** has raised questions and concerns. **Taiwan** has sought clarification on why **Indonesia** participated in the exercise, which took place in waters also close to **Japan** and the **Philippines**. The exercise underscores increasing tensions and shifting military dynamics in the region, particularly in areas strategically important to several nations. The participation of **Indonesia** in such an exercise with **China** could signal evolving security alignments and potentially contribute to a recalibration of regional power balances.
