## November cyclone for the Chennai–Puducherry coast
The Chennai–Puducherry coast has seen developments regarding a November cyclone. Cyclone Akvan was expected to bring rain to Tamil Nadu, with Chennai specifically likely to experience thunderstorms and gusty winds. However, a more recent update indicates a change in the weather outlook. Dry days are now anticipated starting today, November twenty-third, as the low-pressure system associated with the weather disturbance is moving towards Sri Lanka. This suggests that while initial cyclonic activity brought adverse weather, the system's movement away from the coast is leading to a forecast of clearer conditions.
