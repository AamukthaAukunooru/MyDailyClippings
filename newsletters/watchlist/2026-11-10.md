## November cyclone for the Chennai–Puducherry coast
Listeners following the potential November cyclone for the Chennai-Puducherry coast will be interested to hear that the low-pressure system previously observed has now moved towards Sri Lanka. This shift in the weather pattern is expected to bring dry days to Tamil Nadu starting tomorrow, November eleventh. This development significantly reduces the immediate threat of a cyclone impacting the Chennai-Puducherry coastal areas, providing a positive update for residents and authorities concerned about severe weather events.
