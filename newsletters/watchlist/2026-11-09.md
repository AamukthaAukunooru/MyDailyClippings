## November cyclone for the Chennai–Puducherry coast
Dry days are now expected across Tamil Nadu, as the low-pressure system that had been a focus of concern for the Chennai–Puducherry coast has moved away. The system is currently tracking towards Sri Lanka, significantly reducing the immediate threat of a November cyclone impacting the Indian coastline in this region. This development brings relief to residents and authorities who were monitoring the weather patterns closely. The shift of the low-pressure system suggests a period of stable weather conditions for the coming days in Tamil Nadu, moving away from the potential for cyclonic activity that had been anticipated.
