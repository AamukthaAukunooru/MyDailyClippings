## November cyclone for the Chennai–Puducherry coast
A depression is likely to form over the Bay of Bengal tomorrow, November thirteenth, according to reports from November twelfth. This weather system is expected to bring rain to Tamil Nadu until November eighteenth. Rain alerts have also been issued for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana due to the anticipated low pressure system. Earlier reports from November eleventh indicated a low pressure area system could affect the coastal belt, leading to rains in Andhra Pradesh, and the India Meteorological Department had forecast heavy rainfall warnings for several districts in Tamil Nadu.
