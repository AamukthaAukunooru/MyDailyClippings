## The next era of Marvel Digital Comics Webtoons
Marvel Comics and WEBTOON recently showcased their new digital comic service at New York Comic Con. The demonstration on the show floor provided attendees with a look at the upcoming platform. Additionally, a first look at new Webtoon series featuring popular Marvel characters such as the X-Men and Iron Man was revealed. These developments indicate progress in the collaboration between Marvel and WEBTOON to bring new digital comic experiences to readers. While details about a specific launch date or availability in India were not explicitly mentioned in these updates, the demonstrations and first looks suggest the service is moving closer to a public release.
