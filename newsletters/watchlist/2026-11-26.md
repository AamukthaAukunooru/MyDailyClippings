## The next era of Marvel Digital Comics Webtoons
WEBTOON has officially introduced a new feature allowing Marvel Comics readers to "save their favorite titles forever." This development provides readers with a way to permanently access their preferred Marvel series within the WEBTOON platform. While the announcement focuses on the ability to save titles, specific details regarding a new launch date for Marvel Digital Comics Webtoons or any particular focus on the Indian market were not explicitly mentioned in this update. The news highlights an enhancement to the existing WEBTOON platform's functionality for Marvel content.
