## November cyclone for the Chennai–Puducherry coast
Cyclone Akwan has been a significant weather event, initially intensifying into a severe cyclonic storm. Chennai experienced very heavy rain on November 18th and 19th. The cyclone later weakened to a severe cyclonic storm, prompting a yellow alert for Chennai, with heavy rain still expected on November 19th. Light rain was also likely in the Western Ghats districts of Tamil Nadu. Looking ahead, Cyclone Akwan is projected to cross the Odisha coast tomorrow, November 20th, and Chennai is likely to receive more rain on November 23rd and 24th.
